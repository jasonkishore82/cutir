# Phases

Phases 1 to 5 built the lyric video editor. Phases 6 to 10 add movable text, overrides, animation slots, keyframes and transitions. Phases 11 to 30 grow Cutir into a general video editor. See `davinci-research.md` for the workflow research behind them.
